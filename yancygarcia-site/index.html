About Yancy Garcia

Yancy Garcia is the founder of Brevmont, an AI sales assistant built for car dealership reps, and the automotive content creator behind @cardogvlogs. Based in Jackson, Wyoming, Yancy spent years on the front line of automotive sales — closing over $26.8 million in cars — before turning that experience into software that helps dealership teams sell more.

Today he splits his time between building Brevmont and creating daily content that documents the real car-sales business: the deals, the trade-ins, the grind, and the mindset behind it. His work sits at the intersection of automotive retail, AI sales tools, and creator-led brand building.
