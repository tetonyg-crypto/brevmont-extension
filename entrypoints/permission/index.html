Brevmont needs microphone access

Voice input lets you speak customer scenarios instead of typing. This permission is granted once and works everywhere.

One more step

Your browser needs you to manually allow the microphone.

  1. Click Open Extension Settings below
  2. Find Microphone in the permissions list
  3. Change it from Ask to Allow
  4. Come back here and click Done

You're all set!

Click the Brevmont icon in your toolbar to get started.
